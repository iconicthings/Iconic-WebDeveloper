Good to know

Questions clients usually ask.

Can't find your answer here? Reach out directly! I reply within one business day.

01How do you price a project?

It depends on what you need! I offer a fixed price based on your requirements, so you know the exact total cost upfront before we start & no hidden fees or surprises later.

02What's your typical timeline?

It depends on the project! Simple pages, portfolios, or landing pages usually take 3 to 5 days, while larger websites take around 1 to 2 weeks. I’ll give you an exact deadline upfront and keep you updated on the progress every step of the way.

03Do you work with existing teams?

Yes, all the time! As a web development student, I love collaborating. I can easily join your existing project, work with your codebase, and comfortably team up with your designers, developers, or project leads.

04What happens after launch?

You get 30 days of free support after launch for any bug fixes, small tweaks, or adjustments. I'll also guide you on how to easily manage and update your website yourself going forward.

05Are you available right now?

I am! However, to deliver top-tier quality for every client, I only take on a limited number of projects at a time. Send me a quick message, and I’ll confirm my availability within 24 hours.
